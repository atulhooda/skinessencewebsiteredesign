## Frequently asked questions

### Why is young children's skin more vulnerable to medicated creams?

Children have an underdeveloped skin barrier and a higher surface area-to-weight ratio than adults. Because of this, their skin absorbs topical products more readily, increasing the risk of systemic side effects.

### What are the risks of using over-the-counter steroid creams on children?

Inappropriate or prolonged steroid use can cause local damage like skin thinning, bruising, stretch marks, and topical steroid dependence. It can also lead to internal complications such as growth slowing, Cushing's syndrome, and suppressed adrenal function.

### Are routine blood tests necessary to diagnose eczema in Indian children?

No. Expert consensus guidelines from the IADVL recommend against routine serum total IgE testing because it is neither sensitive nor specific. Dermatologists rely instead on detailed clinical examination and specific diagnostic criteria.

### What should parents expect during a paediatric dermatology consultation?

Parents can expect a gentle, empathetic examination tailored to young children. Dermatologists assess skin conditions, prescribe single-agent medical treatments, and may use non-invasive skin surface microscopy to check delicate blood vessels and prevent tissue damage.
