## Preventative Wrinkles Fine Lines: Dynamic Creases Versus Resting Folds

Knowing when to begin skin maintenance starts with understanding how lines form. Every time you smile, laugh, or frown, underlying muscles contract. This movement folds the skin above. In young skin, rich in collagen and elastin, the surface snaps back smoothly when your face rests.

Over time, natural ageing and daily stress reduce skin bounce. When skin folds repeatedly along the same pathways while losing structural support, lines begin to stay. This progression moves through three distinct phases:

- Dynamic Creases: These show up only during active movement, like squinting or raising your eyebrows. They vanish completely once your face rests.
- Transitional Marks: These faint marks linger for a few minutes after facial movement stops. They show that skin bounce is beginning to drop.
- Static Folds: These lines stay visible even when your face is fully relaxed. They reflect broken collagen scaffolding in deeper layers.

Early clinical care is most effective during dynamic and transitional phases. By easing muscle tension and supporting collagen renewal early, dermatologists keep daily mechanical movement from setting into deeper structural creases.

- Strict Sun Protection: Treated skin must be shielded from ultraviolet rays to stop newly formed collagen from degrading.
