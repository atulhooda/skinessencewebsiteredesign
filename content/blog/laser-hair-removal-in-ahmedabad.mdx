## Selecting Wavelengths for Fitzpatrick Types IV and V

In clinical dermatology, hair reduction aims to heat and disable dark, coarse hairs while sparing the surrounding skin ([Laser therapy in skin of colour](https://dermnetnz.org/topics/laser-therapy-in-skin-of-colour)). - Ruby laser (694 nm)
- Alexandrite laser (755 nm)
- Diode laser (800 nm)
- Neodymium-doped yttrium aluminium garnet (Nd:YAG) laser (1064 nm)

For patients with skin of colour, the long-pulsed 1064 nm Nd:YAG laser offers the highest level of safety ([Laser therapy in skin of colour](https://dermnetnz.org/topics/laser-therapy-in-skin-of-colour)). An 800 nm diode laser is reported to be mainly safe with low complication rates ([Laser therapy in skin of colour](https://dermnetnz.org/topics/laser-therapy-in-skin-of-colour)). Even so, the long-pulsed 1064 nm Nd:YAG laser remains recognized as the safest choice for skin of colour ([Laser therapy in skin of colour](https://dermnetnz.org/topics/laser-therapy-in-skin-of-colour)) because its longer wavelength reduces light scatter and achieves deeper tissue penetration ([Evaluation of Long-pulsed 1064 nm Nd:YAG Laser-assisted Hair Remova…](https://jcasonline.com/evaluation-of-long-pulsed-1064-nm-ndyag-laser-assisted-hair-removal-vs-multiple-treatment-sessions-and-different-hair-types-in-indian-patients/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### Which laser wavelength is safest for Indian skin phototypes?

The long-pulsed 1064 nm Nd:YAG laser is considered the safest option for Fitzpatrick skin phototypes IV and V. Its wavelength operates at the tail end of melanin's absorption spectrum, bypassing surface pigment to penetrate deeply and safely target hair follicles.

### How many laser hair removal sessions are typically needed?

Most patients require between 2 and 6 sessions to achieve hair removal, spaced about 4 to 6 weeks apart. This spacing is necessary because lasers only target follicles during their active anagen growth phase.

### Why is an evaluation for PCOD recommended before facial laser hair removal?

Underlying conditions like polycystic ovarian disease (PCOD) frequently cause coarse facial hair along the jawline and chin. Diagnosing hormonal imbalances is vital because patients with PCOD typically need concurrent medical therapy and more sessions to achieve satisfactory results.

### What pre-procedure sun care is required before a session in Ahmedabad?

Patients must avoid sunbathing, indoor tanning, and any outdoor activities that tan the skin in the treatment area. Applying a broad-spectrum sunscreen regularly is essential, as tanned skin significantly increases the risk of thermal burns and hyperpigmentation.
