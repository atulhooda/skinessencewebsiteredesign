## Diagnostic Clarity: Differentiating PIH, PIE, and Acne Scars

Many self-care routines fail because different post-acne blemishes require different care. Using a pigment-lightening product on vascular redness will not produce clear results. A clinical skin assessment separates post-breakout marks into three distinct categories:

### 1\. Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation (PIH) vs. Postinflammatory Erythema (PIE)

Post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation represents post-breakout pigment alteration ([Easy as PIE (Postinflammatory Erythema)](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEvSQNQKiPD8e0jZukAamh1juGDpabAGNbVSWivGA0Oow9vaw9KAU5C30rtjljJ1_qcpu7NkMvx4ZNiD3cWg-KyzOOikzym71ie-wB6J2gzDoxV7_jQrw-okw2NeUlxZhDuhtFfv4Ib)). Superficial peels are considered safe for Indian patients ([Standard guidelines of care for chemical peels](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEHegw2dRAhytz-GhZWXM55Ibkq_DkHFsIanDgptOBVJmHtwXr1YE6ZZ65lQxRM4iKMQGc6A5jQL7jN4kSW2GHWcU0IUdGCphzJiMn1znVKSYF8ZTPlDDG7VX3ZbuaicQ-w9abAAsaNJF26gy2LkCcEaa2pjFmMkg==)). However, peel concentration and contact time must be carefully controlled to avoid irritation that could worsen pigmentation ([Tips for managing post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation of acne](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQGi6E7tMuXgj58BOsRzMRZpGZv1aACI8BQutPqS_w2EPOkcAd2G-hmaAFBcV7dHrb9RiQRC7UNGedqEWdEhjc5PBPRkTIw7c0Zg6FMa_1s-44mjGBw4B7Q-m40bnQe0ah2RzOg_cZqngXZQ-4kqkCzSnw8tUOgBH3o1SsV_5hFZETK8AxdOakvXLMRG)).

### The Importance of Pre-Peel Priming

A clinical peel should not be applied to unprimed skin. Skin priming is essential for at least 2 to 4 weeks before the procedure ([Standard guidelines of care for chemical peels](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEHegw2dRAhytz-GhZWXM55Ibkq_DkHFsIanDgptOBVJmHtwXr1YE6ZZ65lQxRM4iKMQGc6A5jQL7jN4kSW2GHWcU0IUdGCphzJiMn1znVKSYF8ZTPlDDG7VX3ZbuaicQ-w9abAAsaNJF26gy2LkCcEaa2pjFmMkg==)). Priming is performed using broad-spectrum sunscreens, hydroquinone (2–4%), or mild topical agents such as tretinoin, adapalene, or glycolic acid ([Standard guidelines of care for chemical peels](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEHegw2dRAhytz-GhZWXM55Ibkq_DkHFsIanDgptOBVJmHtwXr1YE6ZZ65lQxRM4iKMQGc6A5jQL7jN4kSW2GHWcU0IUdGCphzJiMn1znVKSYF8ZTPlDDG7VX3ZbuaicQ-w9abAAsaNJF26gy2LkCcEaa2pjFmMkg==)). Dermatologists may also prime the skin using kojic acid or glycolic acid in 6–12% concentrations ([Tips for managing post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation of acne](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQGi6E7tMuXgj58BOsRzMRZpGZv1aACI8BQutPqS_w2EPOkcAd2G-hmaAFBcV7dHrb9RiQRC7UNGedqEWdEhjc5PBPRkTIw7c0Zg6FMa_1s-44mjGBw4B7Q-m40bnQe0ah2RzOg_cZqngXZQ-4kqkCzSnw8tUOgBH3o1SsV_5hFZETK8AxdOakvXLMRG)).

Priming serves essential clinical purposes. It facilitates uniform peel penetration, reduces wound healing time, identifies skin intolerance, and lowers the risk of post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation ([Standard guidelines of care for chemical peels](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEHegw2dRAhytz-GhZWXM55Ibkq_DkHFsIanDgptOBVJmHtwXr1YE6ZZ65lQxRM4iKMQGc6A5jQL7jN4kSW2GHWcU0IUdGCphzJiMn1znVKSYF8ZTPlDDG7VX3ZbuaicQ-w9abAAsaNJF26gy2LkCcEaa2pjFmMkg==)).

### Peeling Depth and Clinical Safety Limits

Peel depth must be strictly calibrated for Indian skin. Superficial peels are considered safe for Indian patients, but medium-depth peels carry a high risk of PIH and require great caution ([Standard guidelines of care for chemical peels](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEHegw2dRAhytz-GhZWXM55Ibkq_DkHFsIanDgptOBVJmHtwXr1YE6ZZ65lQxRM4iKMQGc6A5jQL7jN4kSW2GHWcU0IUdGCphzJiMn1znVKSYF8ZTPlDDG7VX3ZbuaicQ-w9abAAsaNJF26gy2LkCcEaa2pjFmMkg==)). Deep phenol peels are not recommended for Indian skin because of the risk of long-lasting pigmentary alterations ([Standard guidelines of care for chemical peels](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEHegw2dRAhytz-GhZWXM55Ibkq_DkHFsIanDgptOBVJmHtwXr1YE6ZZ65lQxRM4iKMQGc6A5jQL7jN4kSW2GHWcU0IUdGCphzJiMn1znVKSYF8ZTPlDDG7VX3ZbuaicQ-w9abAAsaNJF26gy2LkCcEaa2pjFmMkg==)).

### Procedural End Points and Monitoring

During an in-clinic peel, the dermatologist watches closely for specific procedural stopping points:

- Glycolic Acid Peels: The acid is typically neutralised after a set time, usually around 3 minutes ([Standard guidelines of care for chemical peels](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEHegw2dRAhytz-GhZWXM55Ibkq_DkHFsIanDgptOBVJmHtwXr1YE6ZZ65lQxRM4iKMQGc6A5jQL7jN4kSW2GHWcU0IUdGCphzJiMn1znVKSYF8ZTPlDDG7VX3ZbuaicQ-w9abAAsaNJF26gy2LkCcEaa2pjFmMkg==)). However, if early redness or epidermolysis appears—seen as a greyish-white surface or tiny blisters—the peel is neutralised immediately ([Standard guidelines of care for chemical peels](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEHegw2dRAhytz-GhZWXM55Ibkq_DkHFsIanDgptOBVJmHtwXr1YE6ZZ65lQxRM4iKMQGc6A5jQL7jN4kSW2GHWcU0IUdGCphzJiMn1znVKSYF8ZTPlDDG7VX3ZbuaicQ-w9abAAsaNJF26gy2LkCcEaa2pjFmMkg==)).
- Salicylic Acid Peels: Salicylic acid crystallises on the skin surface, forming a pseudofrost ([Standard guidelines of care for chemical peels](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEHegw2dRAhytz-GhZWXM55Ibkq_DkHFsIanDgptOBVJmHtwXr1YE6ZZ65lQxRM4iKMQGc6A5jQL7jN4kSW2GHWcU0IUdGCphzJiMn1znVKSYF8ZTPlDDG7VX3ZbuaicQ-w9abAAsaNJF26gy2LkCcEaa2pjFmMkg==)). This pseudofrost is thoroughly washed off with water after 3 to 5 minutes ([Standard guidelines of care for chemical peels](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEHegw2dRAhytz-GhZWXM55Ibkq_DkHFsIanDgptOBVJmHtwXr1YE6ZZ65lQxRM4iKMQGc6A5jQL7jN4kSW2GHWcU0IUdGCphzJiMn1znVKSYF8ZTPlDDG7VX3ZbuaicQ-w9abAAsaNJF26gy2LkCcEaa2pjFmMkg==)).
- Post-Peel Care: Patients must apply bland moisturisers and sunscreens until peeling is complete ([Standard guidelines of care for chemical peels](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEHegw2dRAhytz-GhZWXM55Ibkq_DkHFsIanDgptOBVJmHtwXr1YE6ZZ65lQxRM4iKMQGc6A5jQL7jN4kSW2GHWcU0IUdGCphzJiMn1znVKSYF8ZTPlDDG7VX3ZbuaicQ-w9abAAsaNJF26gy2LkCcEaa2pjFmMkg==)). Redness lasting more than three weeks indicates early scarring and must be treated promptly with potent topical corticosteroids for up to two weeks ([Standard guidelines of care for chemical peels](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEHegw2dRAhytz-GhZWXM55Ibkq_DkHFsIanDgptOBVJmHtwXr1YE6ZZ65lQxRM4iKMQGc6A5jQL7jN4kSW2GHWcU0IUdGCphzJiMn1znVKSYF8ZTPlDDG7VX3ZbuaicQ-w9abAAsaNJF26gy2LkCcEaa2pjFmMkg==)).

### The Subcellular Mechanism of Laser Toning

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation and acne scars?

Post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH) and postinflammatory erythema (PIE) represent temporary colour changes that tend to resolve over time. In contrast, true acne scars involve permanent structural and textural changes in the skin.

### Why is skin priming necessary before a chemical peel?

Priming the skin for two to four weeks ensures uniform peel penetration, shortens healing time, detects skin intolerance, and significantly reduces the risk of post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation.

### How does laser toning treat deep dermal post-acne marks?

Low-dose Q-switched Nd:YAG laser toning penetrates the dermis to break up melanin granules inside melanosomes with minimal epidermal disruption, clearing deep pigment safely.

### Why are standard untinted sunscreens insufficient for preventing post-acne marks in Indian skin?

Standard untinted sunscreens do not block visible light or blue light, which make up roughly half of solar radiation and directly stimulate melanocytes via opsin-3 receptors. Tinted sunscreens containing iron oxides block visible light and shield against persistent dark spots.
