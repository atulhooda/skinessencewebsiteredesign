## Frequently asked questions

### When can I wash my hair after a hair transplant?

Recipient scalp washing begins between Days 3 and 5 using a hands-free cup-pour technique with diluted gentle shampoo and lukewarm water, avoiding direct shower pressure. From Day 6 onward, an emollient can be used to soften scabs, and gentle circular motions with fingertip pads may be introduced.

### How should I sleep after undergoing a hair transplant?

Patients should sleep on their back with the upper body elevated at roughly 30 to 45 degrees using a neck pillow for the first 10 nights. This positioning prevents nocturnal friction against the recipient area and helps drain fluid from local anaesthetic solutions to reduce facial swelling.

### Why does hair shed a few weeks after a transplant?

Temporary shedding between Weeks 2 and 4, known as shock loss or anagen effluvium, is a normal response to surgical stress. While the hair shafts shed as follicles temporarily enter a resting phase, the underlying follicular root structures remain intact beneath the skin to produce new growth starting around Months 3 to 4.

### How does Pune's climate affect hair transplant recovery?

Warm daytime temperatures and urban commuting in Pune can trigger scalp perspiration, which risks causing folliculitis—an inflammation producing spots at the base of transplanted hairs. Patients should avoid heavy exertion, stay in cool shaded areas, and keep the scalp clean and ventilated during the initial weeks.
