## Recognising Viral Warts on Children's Hands, Knees, and Feet

Warts often show up in areas exposed to minor scrapes, cuts, and friction during school sports and games. Spotting them early helps parents take action before they enlarge or multiply:

## The Hazards of Unsupervised Home Remedies and Acid Solutions

When a wart appears, parents may feel tempted to try over-the-counter acid bottles, garlic pastes, or internet tricks. For young skin, home remedies often cause far more trouble than the wart itself.

Delicate skin burns easily. Strong acid liquids or harsh pastes can damage healthy skin around the wart. These burns can cause lingering redness, dark or light patches, and lasting scars. Indian skin tones are particularly prone to post-inflammatory marks that take months to fade.

Attempting to cut, pare, or scrape a wart at home is also dangerous. Viral warts contain tiny blood vessels. Cutting them causes bleeding and spreads viral particles to nearby skin, which can cause additional warts to develop. Scraping also breaks the skin barrier and invites bacterial infections. A clinical consultation provides a safe assessment without the risk of burns or cuts.
