## Session Spacing and Natural Follicular Growth Cycles

Patients often ask why laser hair reduction cannot finish in two or three visits. The reason is hair biology. Hair follicles move through three recurring stages: anagen (active growth), catagen (regression), and telogen (resting).

Laser energy targets follicles that contain active melanin connected to the hair bulb. This connection only exists during the anagen phase. However, at any given time, only 20% to 30% of hairs in a treatment area are in anagen ([Laser Hair Removal: How It Works, What to Expect & Results](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQGu-4BvkGMMSKknFjkdMhvsQiJeVaW8ivPrJ8qULIt2CJIYVUrSNhjZ1ikAPizVPG530fyx7O7MdhO3379J3fq0MFicBNfaZcJzBgWT3XW8__-ma2f6TV9e8iyJeEfKOIO3y-jU-6c0jADPrbszp6S3X_mHRiQd_zbB)). The rest are resting or shedding. These dormant hairs do not respond to laser energy until they re-enter active growth.

Because only some follicles are receptive during a single appointment, achieving significant hair reduction requires 6 to 8 sessions spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart ([Laser Hair Removal: How It Works, What to Expect & Results](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQGu-4BvkGMMSKknFjkdMhvsQiJeVaW8ivPrJ8qULIt2CJIYVUrSNhjZ1ikAPizVPG530fyx7O7MdhO3379J3fq0MFicBNfaZcJzBgWT3XW8__-ma2f6TV9e8iyJeEfKOIO3y-jU-6c0jADPrbszp6S3X_mHRiQd_zbB)). ## Pre-Treatment Preparation and Post-Laser Aftercare Guidelines

Achieving smooth skin without pigment changes requires good preparation and consistent aftercare. Proper skin care ensures that laser energy targets the follicle without surface interference.

Patients should follow key pre-treatment guidelines before each appointment:

- Shaving: Shave the treatment zone closely about 24 hours before the session. Long hair shafts on the surface can singe and increase the risk of superficial skin burns.
- No Plucking or Waxing: Do not wax, pluck, or thread for at least 4 weeks prior to treatment. These methods remove the hair root, eliminating the melanin target needed for the laser.
- Sun Protection: Avoid intentional sun exposure and tanning in Pune's warm climate. A fresh tan raises epidermal melanin levels, increasing the risk of burns and hyperpigmentation.

After a session, the skin barrier needs gentle care. Key post-treatment steps include:

- Thermal Care: Avoid hot showers, steam rooms, saunas, and heavy exercise that causes sweating for 24 to 48 hours to minimize irritation.
- Skin Barrier Support: Use mild, fragrance-free moisturizers and apply broad-spectrum sunscreen to all treated areas exposed to daylight.
- No Scrubs or Acids: Pause physical body scrubs and chemical exfoliants, such as glycolic or salicylic acids, for several days to protect the skin barrier.
- Managing Concurrent Conditions: Patients with body acne, folliculitis, or post-inflammatory marks should consult a dermatologist before laser sessions. Laser reduction helps prevent recurring ingrown hairs, but active bacterial acne requires medical care to keep the skin calm.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why are diode and Nd:YAG lasers preferred for Indian skin?

Indian skin typically falls into Fitzpatrick types IV through VI, which have higher epidermal melanin. ### How many sessions are typically required for full body laser hair removal?

Significant hair reduction generally requires 6 to 8 sessions spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart. This spacing is necessary because lasers only target follicles in the active anagen growth phase, which accounts for only 20% to 30% of hairs at any given time.

### What is paradoxical hypertrichosis and how is it prevented?

Paradoxical hypertrichosis is an uncommon side effect where fine hairs are stimulated to grow thicker, often caused by subtherapeutic heat or hormonal factors. Dermatologists manage and prevent it by avoiding weak energy settings, calibrating fluences, using multiple passes, and addressing underlying hormonal issues.

### Can I wax or pluck my hair before a laser hair removal session?

No, you must avoid plucking, waxing, or threading for at least 4 weeks before a session because these methods remove the hair root that the laser needs to target. Patients should shave the treatment area closely about 24 hours prior to their appointment instead.

This information is general and not a substitute for a consultation. Results vary from person to person.
