Dark patches and stubborn marks are very common on Indian skin. Melasma, sun spots, and dark blemishes often cause frustration. People with Indian skin tones usually have Fitzpatrick skin types IV to VI. This skin type has active pigment cells. These cells react quickly to sunlight, hormonal changes, and skin irritation. In sunny cities like Pune, daily UV exposure can make these dark marks worse. As a result, many patients look for safe ways to clear their skin.

Patients often compare two main clinic options: chemical peels and laser toning. Both treatments are widely discussed in clinics across Kalyani Nagar and Pune. Yet, they work in completely different ways. Chemical peels promote exfoliation of the outer skin layer to speed up epidermal renewal ([Comparative efficacy of chemical peels and laser treatments in mela…](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQG9S0aeSo8yVefjuXHrs-EeEMFUceJkNZ4EnxC5-r5F6pVSnGVMaLQHyu-DPywBeQVqWkS4VvtoRYXCH8T3LFM-SbQN486Drb7zazyWS7NvloYAzkRiS9H0yttpa0DCn0PMwxcjiw26dFdS5rKxmzr0hffq2ReZYObSXXY00ig=)). In contrast, lasers target melanin selectively across depths through photothermolysis ([Comparative efficacy of chemical peels and laser treatments in mela…](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQG9S0aeSo8yVefjuXHrs-EeEMFUceJkNZ4EnxC5-r5F6pVSnGVMaLQHyu-DPywBeQVqWkS4VvtoRYXCH8T3LFM-SbQN486Drb7zazyWS7NvloYAzkRiS9H0yttpa0DCn0PMwxcjiw26dFdS5rKxmzr0hffq2ReZYObSXXY00ig=)). Choosing between them is not about following cosmetic trends. Instead, it requires an accurate assessment of how deep the pigment lies within the skin.

## Chemical Peels vs Laser Toning: Why Pigment Depth Matters

Before choosing a procedure, a dermatologist checks the depth of the pigment. Melasma and hyperpigmentation can sit in the upper layer, the deeper layer, or both. Evaluating whether melanin sits in superficial or deeper layers helps guide the clinical strategy.

Epidermal pigment sits in the superficial layers of the skin. This includes surface sun tanning, shallow freckles, and recent [post-acne marks](/blog/post-acne-pigmentation-treatment-kalyani-nagar). Chemical peels promote exfoliation of the stratum corneum, speeding up epidermal turnover to lessen melanin in these superficial layers ([Comparative efficacy of chemical peels and laser treatments in mela…](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQG9S0aeSo8yVefjuXHrs-EeEMFUceJkNZ4EnxC5-r5F6pVSnGVMaLQHyu-DPywBeQVqWkS4VvtoRYXCH8T3LFM-SbQN486Drb7zazyWS7NvloYAzkRiS9H0yttpa0DCn0PMwxcjiw26dFdS5rKxmzr0hffq2ReZYObSXXY00ig=)). When pigment is close to the surface, accelerating cell renewal is often enough to brighten the skin.

In contrast, dermal pigment lies much deeper in the skin. Here, melanin granules sit below the dermo-epidermal junction. Deep-seated melasma and grey-brown patches fall into this group. Dermal melanoses rarely respond to superficial peels or topical medical therapy ([Optimizing Q-switched lasers for melasma and acquired dermal melanoses](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQECA02B62yM65jUc6i8CnA6DSiNms8MvuNTLwZZtcb1XIldTNdlgkz26bfBqPuDcnCOid_4XPnWbtq0FzE8wfmU6NNu2FkvyHzKhs_vN6kcHtbpIrW69rJr_zpI_e0KS9eNKAZV-eDhbnrI2zJ-gZRGb_VUFO7mmvMcSlim5HVhzva0YsNaiW8N89Yk3bn7)). Chemical peels applied at safe surface depths cannot reach these deep pigment deposits.

Targeted laser systems offer a clear advantage for deep pigment. A Q-switched laser operating at a 1064 nm wavelength penetrates deeply into the dermis while sparing the epidermis ([Optimizing Q-switched lasers for melasma and acquired dermal melanoses](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQECA02B62yM65jUc6i8CnA6DSiNms8MvuNTLwZZtcb1XIldTNdlgkz26bfBqPuDcnCOid_4XPnWbtq0FzE8wfmU6NNu2FkvyHzKhs_vN6kcHtbpIrW69rJr_zpI_e0KS9eNKAZV-eDhbnrI2zJ-gZRGb_VUFO7mmvMcSlim5HVhzva0YsNaiW8N89Yk3bn7)). This allows it to play a significant role in managing deeper dermal melanoses ([Optimizing Q-switched lasers for melasma and acquired dermal melanoses](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQECA02B62yM65jUc6i8CnA6DSiNms8MvuNTLwZZtcb1XIldTNdlgkz26bfBqPuDcnCOid_4XPnWbtq0FzE8wfmU6NNu2FkvyHzKhs_vN6kcHtbpIrW69rJr_zpI_e0KS9eNKAZV-eDhbnrI2zJ-gZRGb_VUFO7mmvMcSlim5HVhzva0YsNaiW8N89Yk3bn7)). In general, lasers can treat both epidermal and dermal components of melasma more rapidly than chemical peels ([Comparative efficacy of chemical peels and laser treatments in mela…](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQG9S0aeSo8yVefjuXHrs-EeEMFUceJkNZ4EnxC5-r5F6pVSnGVMaLQHyu-DPywBeQVqWkS4VvtoRYXCH8T3LFM-SbQN486Drb7zazyWS7NvloYAzkRiS9H0yttpa0DCn0PMwxcjiw26dFdS5rKxmzr0hffq2ReZYObSXXY00ig=)). Furthermore, fractional lasers show particular effectiveness for mixed melasma ([Comparative efficacy of chemical peels and laser treatments in mela…](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQG9S0aeSo8yVefjuXHrs-EeEMFUceJkNZ4EnxC5-r5F6pVSnGVMaLQHyu-DPywBeQVqWkS4VvtoRYXCH8T3LFM-SbQN486Drb7zazyWS7NvloYAzkRiS9H0yttpa0DCn0PMwxcjiw26dFdS5rKxmzr0hffq2ReZYObSXXY00ig=)).

## Mechanism of Action: Acid Exfoliation Versus Laser Photothermolysis

Understanding how each treatment interacts with the skin helps patients make informed choices. Both methods aim to clear pigment, but their physical actions are very different.

### Chemical Peels: Exfoliation and Surface Turnover

Chemical peels use carefully formulated clinical acids. Common examples include glycolic acid, salicylic acid, and trichloroacetic acid (TCA) ([Comparative efficacy of chemical peels and laser treatments in mela…](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQG9S0aeSo8yVefjuXHrs-EeEMFUceJkNZ4EnxC5-r5F6pVSnGVMaLQHyu-DPywBeQVqWkS4VvtoRYXCH8T3LFM-SbQN486Drb7zazyWS7NvloYAzkRiS9H0yttpa0DCn0PMwxcjiw26dFdS5rKxmzr0hffq2ReZYObSXXY00ig=)). These solutions loosen the bonds between dead surface skin cells. Chemical peels promote exfoliation of the stratum corneum, facilitating epidermal turnover and reducing melanin concentration in the superficial layers of the skin ([Comparative efficacy of chemical peels and laser treatments in mela…](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQG9S0aeSo8yVefjuXHrs-EeEMFUceJkNZ4EnxC5-r5F6pVSnGVMaLQHyu-DPywBeQVqWkS4VvtoRYXCH8T3LFM-SbQN486Drb7zazyWS7NvloYAzkRiS9H0yttpa0DCn0PMwxcjiw26dFdS5rKxmzr0hffq2ReZYObSXXY00ig=)). Aside from lightening surface dark spots, peels also smooth rough skin and improve overall texture.

### Laser Toning: Subcellular Photothermolysis

Laser toning works through a process called [subcellular selective photothermolysis](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQECA02B62yM65jUc6i8CnA6DSiNms8MvuNTLwZZtcb1XIldTNdlgkz26bfBqPuDcnCOid_4XPnWbtq0FzE8wfmU6NNu2FkvyHzKhs_vN6kcHtbpIrW69rJr_zpI_e0KS9eNKAZV-eDhbnrI2zJ-gZRGb_VUFO7mmvMcSlim5HVhzva0YsNaiW8N89Yk3bn7) ([Optimizing Q-switched lasers for melasma and acquired dermal melanoses](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQECA02B62yM65jUc6i8CnA6DSiNms8MvuNTLwZZtcb1XIldTNdlgkz26bfBqPuDcnCOid_4XPnWbtq0FzE8wfmU6NNu2FkvyHzKhs_vN6kcHtbpIrW69rJr_zpI_e0KS9eNKAZV-eDhbnrI2zJ-gZRGb_VUFO7mmvMcSlim5HVhzva0YsNaiW8N89Yk3bn7)). The treatment uses a Q-switched laser with ultrashort pulses of about 5 nanoseconds, high peak power, and a flat-top beam profile ([Optimizing Q-switched lasers for melasma and acquired dermal melanoses](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQECA02B62yM65jUc6i8CnA6DSiNms8MvuNTLwZZtcb1XIldTNdlgkz26bfBqPuDcnCOid_4XPnWbtq0FzE8wfmU6NNu2FkvyHzKhs_vN6kcHtbpIrW69rJr_zpI_e0KS9eNKAZV-eDhbnrI2zJ-gZRGb_VUFO7mmvMcSlim5HVhzva0YsNaiW8N89Yk3bn7)). This optical energy selectively breaks down melanin and Stage IV melanosomes inside the cells ([Optimizing Q-switched lasers for melasma and acquired dermal melanoses](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQECA02B62yM65jUc6i8CnA6DSiNms8MvuNTLwZZtcb1XIldTNdlgkz26bfBqPuDcnCOid_4XPnWbtq0FzE8wfmU6NNu2FkvyHzKhs_vN6kcHtbpIrW69rJr_zpI_e0KS9eNKAZV-eDhbnrI2zJ-gZRGb_VUFO7mmvMcSlim5HVhzva0YsNaiW8N89Yk3bn7)).

This process selectively destroys melanin and Stage IV melanosomes while keeping target cells alive ([Optimizing Q-switched lasers for melasma and acquired dermal melanoses](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQECA02B62yM65jUc6i8CnA6DSiNms8MvuNTLwZZtcb1XIldTNdlgkz26bfBqPuDcnCOid_4XPnWbtq0FzE8wfmU6NNu2FkvyHzKhs_vN6kcHtbpIrW69rJr_zpI_e0KS9eNKAZV-eDhbnrI2zJ-gZRGb_VUFO7mmvMcSlim5HVhzva0YsNaiW8N89Yk3bn7)). It also reduces melanocyte dendrites, which helps minimise inflammation ([Optimizing Q-switched lasers for melasma and acquired dermal melanoses](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQECA02B62yM65jUc6i8CnA6DSiNms8MvuNTLwZZtcb1XIldTNdlgkz26bfBqPuDcnCOid_4XPnWbtq0FzE8wfmU6NNu2FkvyHzKhs_vN6kcHtbpIrW69rJr_zpI_e0KS9eNKAZV-eDhbnrI2zJ-gZRGb_VUFO7mmvMcSlim5HVhzva0YsNaiW8N89Yk3bn7)). Through these different mechanisms, chemical peels facilitate stratum corneum exfoliation and accelerate epidermal renewal ([Comparative efficacy of chemical peels and laser treatments in mela…](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQG9S0aeSo8yVefjuXHrs-EeEMFUceJkNZ4EnxC5-r5F6pVSnGVMaLQHyu-DPywBeQVqWkS4VvtoRYXCH8T3LFM-SbQN486Drb7zazyWS7NvloYAzkRiS9H0yttpa0DCn0PMwxcjiw26dFdS5rKxmzr0hffq2ReZYObSXXY00ig=)). In contrast, laser toning selectively targets and fragments melanin inside cells while keeping the host cells alive ([Optimizing Q-switched lasers for melasma and acquired dermal melanoses](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQECA02B62yM65jUc6i8CnA6DSiNms8MvuNTLwZZtcb1XIldTNdlgkz26bfBqPuDcnCOid_4XPnWbtq0FzE8wfmU6NNu2FkvyHzKhs_vN6kcHtbpIrW69rJr_zpI_e0KS9eNKAZV-eDhbnrI2zJ-gZRGb_VUFO7mmvMcSlim5HVhzva0YsNaiW8N89Yk3bn7)).

## Procedure Experience, Session Timelines, and Recovery Realities

Neither chemical peels nor laser toning clears pigmentation overnight. Indian skin requires gentle, staged sessions. Moving too fast can irritate sensitive pigment cells and trigger unwanted darkening.

### What to Expect During a Chemical Peel

During a chemical peel, the doctor applies the acid solution evenly across clean skin. Patients usually feel a warm tingling or prickling sensation for several minutes. The solution is then neutralised. Over the next few days, the skin may feel slightly tight. Mild flaking or light peeling often follows and settles within a few days.

Clinical studies confirm the value of [serial chemical peels for Indian skin](https://jcasonline.com/content/173/2012/5/4/img/JCAS-5-247-g003.png) ([Chemical Peels for Melasma in Dark-Skinned Patients](https://jcasonline.com/content/173/2012/5/4/img/JCAS-5-247-g003.png)). In a study of 40 Indian women, 20% to 35% glycolic acid on one side of the face was compared to 10% to 20% TCA on the other side ([Chemical Peels for Melasma in Dark-Skinned Patients](https://jcasonline.com/content/173/2012/5/4/img/JCAS-5-247-g003.png)). Both sides showed about 75% improvement ([Chemical Peels for Melasma in Dark-Skinned Patients](https://jcasonline.com/content/173/2012/5/4/img/JCAS-5-247-g003.png)). In another study of 100 Indian patients comparing 55% to 75% glycolic acid to 10% to 15% TCA, the TCA group showed a faster response but had more side effects ([Chemical Peels for Melasma in Dark-Skinned Patients](https://jcasonline.com/content/173/2012/5/4/img/JCAS-5-247-g003.png)). Furthermore, adding serial glycolic acid peels to Kligman's formula decreased the Melasma Area Severity Index faster than using Kligman's formula alone in 40 Indian patients ([Chemical Peels for Melasma in Dark-Skinned Patients](https://jcasonline.com/content/173/2012/5/4/img/JCAS-5-247-g003.png)).

### What to Expect During Laser Toning

Laser toning causes minimal discomfort during the session. Patients often describe the feeling as tiny, warm pinpricks on the skin. Because the energy settings remain low, the procedure does not blister or break the skin surface.

A standard clinical protocol uses a Q-switched 1064 nm Nd:YAG laser ([Optimizing Q-switched lasers for melasma and acquired dermal melanoses](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQECA02B62yM65jUc6i8CnA6DSiNms8MvuNTLwZZtcb1XIldTNdlgkz26bfBqPuDcnCOid_4XPnWbtq0FzE8wfmU6NNu2FkvyHzKhs_vN6kcHtbpIrW69rJr_zpI_e0KS9eNKAZV-eDhbnrI2zJ-gZRGb_VUFO7mmvMcSlim5HVhzva0YsNaiW8N89Yk3bn7)). The settings typically feature a 10 mm spot size, a fluence of 1.2 J/cm², and a 10 Hz repetition rate ([Optimizing Q-switched lasers for melasma and acquired dermal melanoses](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQECA02B62yM65jUc6i8CnA6DSiNms8MvuNTLwZZtcb1XIldTNdlgkz26bfBqPuDcnCOid_4XPnWbtq0FzE8wfmU6NNu2FkvyHzKhs_vN6kcHtbpIrW69rJr_zpI_e0KS9eNKAZV-eDhbnrI2zJ-gZRGb_VUFO7mmvMcSlim5HVhzva0YsNaiW8N89Yk3bn7)). The practitioner uses multiple passes across the area ([Optimizing Q-switched lasers for melasma and acquired dermal melanoses](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQECA02B62yM65jUc6i8CnA6DSiNms8MvuNTLwZZtcb1XIldTNdlgkz26bfBqPuDcnCOid_4XPnWbtq0FzE8wfmU6NNu2FkvyHzKhs_vN6kcHtbpIrW69rJr_zpI_e0KS9eNKAZV-eDhbnrI2zJ-gZRGb_VUFO7mmvMcSlim5HVhzva0YsNaiW8N89Yk3bn7)). The desired clinical endpoint is erythema without frosting ([Optimizing Q-switched lasers for melasma and acquired dermal melanoses](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQECA02B62yM65jUc6i8CnA6DSiNms8MvuNTLwZZtcb1XIldTNdlgkz26bfBqPuDcnCOid_4XPnWbtq0FzE8wfmU6NNu2FkvyHzKhs_vN6kcHtbpIrW69rJr_zpI_e0KS9eNKAZV-eDhbnrI2zJ-gZRGb_VUFO7mmvMcSlim5HVhzva0YsNaiW8N89Yk3bn7)). Dermatologists typically deliver this treatment once every 2 weeks for 6 to 10 sessions ([Optimizing Q-switched lasers for melasma and acquired dermal melanoses](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQECA02B62yM65jUc6i8CnA6DSiNms8MvuNTLwZZtcb1XIldTNdlgkz26bfBqPuDcnCOid_4XPnWbtq0FzE8wfmU6NNu2FkvyHzKhs_vN6kcHtbpIrW69rJr_zpI_e0KS9eNKAZV-eDhbnrI2zJ-gZRGb_VUFO7mmvMcSlim5HVhzva0YsNaiW8N89Yk3bn7)). Patients can resume their normal daily activities right away, provided they protect their skin from the sun.

## Safety in Melanin-Rich Skin: Preventing PIH and Mottled Hypopigmentation

Treating darker skin requires careful attention to safety. Melanin-rich skin responds strongly to heat, trauma, and irritation. Any excess inflammation can cause melanocytes to overproduce pigment. This leads to post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH).

Clinical evidence shows clear differences between modalities. Lasers show higher efficacy in reducing melasma severity, with a pooled standardized mean difference of 0.82 compared to 0.65 for chemical peels ([Comparative efficacy of chemical peels and laser treatments in mela…](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQG9S0aeSo8yVefjuXHrs-EeEMFUceJkNZ4EnxC5-r5F6pVSnGVMaLQHyu-DPywBeQVqWkS4VvtoRYXCH8T3LFM-SbQN486Drb7zazyWS7NvloYAzkRiS9H0yttpa0DCn0PMwxcjiw26dFdS5rKxmzr0hffq2ReZYObSXXY00ig=)). However, lasers also carry a higher risk of PIH, especially in darker skin types (Fitzpatrick IV to VI) ([Comparative efficacy of chemical peels and laser treatments in mela…](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQG9S0aeSo8yVefjuXHrs-EeEMFUceJkNZ4EnxC5-r5F6pVSnGVMaLQHyu-DPywBeQVqWkS4VvtoRYXCH8T3LFM-SbQN486Drb7zazyWS7NvloYAzkRiS9H0yttpa0DCn0PMwxcjiw26dFdS5rKxmzr0hffq2ReZYObSXXY00ig=)). In fact, up to 25% of patients treated with Q-switched lasers develop PIH due to melanocyte activation triggered by inflammatory injury ([Comparative efficacy of chemical peels and laser treatments in mela…](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQG9S0aeSo8yVefjuXHrs-EeEMFUceJkNZ4EnxC5-r5F6pVSnGVMaLQHyu-DPywBeQVqWkS4VvtoRYXCH8T3LFM-SbQN486Drb7zazyWS7NvloYAzkRiS9H0yttpa0DCn0PMwxcjiw26dFdS5rKxmzr0hffq2ReZYObSXXY00ig=)). In a study of 18 Korean patients treated with a 1550 nm fractional laser versus a 15% TCA peel, 28% of patients developed PIH ([Chemical Peels for Melasma in Dark-Skinned Patients](https://jcasonline.com/content/173/2012/5/4/img/JCAS-5-247-g003.png)).

Laser toning also carries a risk of mottled hypopigmentation if performed too aggressively. High fluences, small spot sizes, or overly frequent sessions can cause patchy light spots ([Optimizing Q-switched lasers for melasma and acquired dermal melanoses](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQECA02B62yM65jUc6i8CnA6DSiNms8MvuNTLwZZtcb1XIldTNdlgkz26bfBqPuDcnCOid_4XPnWbtq0FzE8wfmU6NNu2FkvyHzKhs_vN6kcHtbpIrW69rJr_zpI_e0KS9eNKAZV-eDhbnrI2zJ-gZRGb_VUFO7mmvMcSlim5HVhzva0YsNaiW8N89Yk3bn7)). Histological analysis shows that this mottled hypopigmentation does not stem from a loss of melanocyte count ([Optimizing Q-switched lasers for melasma and acquired dermal melanoses](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQECA02B62yM65jUc6i8CnA6DSiNms8MvuNTLwZZtcb1XIldTNdlgkz26bfBqPuDcnCOid_4XPnWbtq0FzE8wfmU6NNu2FkvyHzKhs_vN6kcHtbpIrW69rJr_zpI_e0KS9eNKAZV-eDhbnrI2zJ-gZRGb_VUFO7mmvMcSlim5HVhzva0YsNaiW8N89Yk3bn7)). Instead, melanocytes are functionally downregulated and their melanosomes are damaged ([Optimizing Q-switched lasers for melasma and acquired dermal melanoses](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQECA02B62yM65jUc6i8CnA6DSiNms8MvuNTLwZZtcb1XIldTNdlgkz26bfBqPuDcnCOid_4XPnWbtq0FzE8wfmU6NNu2FkvyHzKhs_vN6kcHtbpIrW69rJr_zpI_e0KS9eNKAZV-eDhbnrI2zJ-gZRGb_VUFO7mmvMcSlim5HVhzva0YsNaiW8N89Yk3bn7)).

To lower these risks in darker skin, qualified dermatologists follow key safeguards:

- Priming the skin with skin-lightening agents for 2 to 3 weeks prior to treatment ([Optimizing Q-switched lasers for melasma and acquired dermal melanoses](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQECA02B62yM65jUc6i8CnA6DSiNms8MvuNTLwZZtcb1XIldTNdlgkz26bfBqPuDcnCOid_4XPnWbtq0FzE8wfmU6NNu2FkvyHzKhs_vN6kcHtbpIrW69rJr_zpI_e0KS9eNKAZV-eDhbnrI2zJ-gZRGb_VUFO7mmvMcSlim5HVhzva0YsNaiW8N89Yk3bn7)).
- Performing test spots before proceeding with full treatment ([Optimizing Q-switched lasers for melasma and acquired dermal melanoses](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQECA02B62yM65jUc6i8CnA6DSiNms8MvuNTLwZZtcb1XIldTNdlgkz26bfBqPuDcnCOid_4XPnWbtq0FzE8wfmU6NNu2FkvyHzKhs_vN6kcHtbpIrW69rJr_zpI_e0KS9eNKAZV-eDhbnrI2zJ-gZRGb_VUFO7mmvMcSlim5HVhzva0YsNaiW8N89Yk3bn7)).
- Using large spot sizes of 8 to 10 mm and low fluences ([Optimizing Q-switched lasers for melasma and acquired dermal melanoses](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQECA02B62yM65jUc6i8CnA6DSiNms8MvuNTLwZZtcb1XIldTNdlgkz26bfBqPuDcnCOid_4XPnWbtq0FzE8wfmU6NNu2FkvyHzKhs_vN6kcHtbpIrW69rJr_zpI_e0KS9eNKAZV-eDhbnrI2zJ-gZRGb_VUFO7mmvMcSlim5HVhzva0YsNaiW8N89Yk3bn7)).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the main difference between chemical peels and laser toning?

Chemical peels promote surface exfoliation to speed up skin turnover and reduce superficial epidermal melanin, whereas laser toning uses ultrashort laser pulses to penetrate deeper and break down melanin inside cells without destroying them.

### Can chemical peels treat deep dermal pigmentation?

Dermal pigmentation lies below the dermo-epidermal junction and rarely responds to superficial chemical peels. Laser systems like the Q-switched 1064 nm laser are needed to reach and treat these deeper pigment deposits.

### Why does Indian skin require special precautions during laser toning?

Melanin-rich skin responds strongly to heat and inflammation, which carries a risk of post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation or mottled hypopigmentation. To ensure safety, dermatologists use low fluences, larger spot sizes, proper skin priming, and adequate intervals between sessions.

### Can chemical peels and laser toning be combined?

Yes. Dermatologists often sequence chemical peels before laser toning to clear initial surface pigment, which allows more uniform laser penetration and reduces the laser energy needed, lowering the risk of post-treatment darkening.
